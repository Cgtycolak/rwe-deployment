Gaps

Three different situations, only one of which is a real shortfall.

Correctly absent

38 1,524 MWm licensed

Licensed but not yet in service — the EPDK file lists zero operating capacity. They produce nothing, so their absence from the map is right.

Matched, never reported

26 392 MW operating

Found in EPİAŞ, but no output in three years, so neither class applies. Mostly small. The exception is Ermenek (306 MW), which likely reports under another name.

The real shortfall

46 1,217 MW operating

In service and generating, but not yet matched to an EPİAŞ record. Around 30 appear in the day-ahead (uevcb) list under a different name.

What closes the gap

These plants are not missing from EPİAŞ — they are filed under different names. Yedigöze proved it: EPİAŞ carries it as SANİBEY HES. It is now mapped by hand and contributes its full 317 MW, which is why the missing pool fell from 1,534 MW to 1,217 MW.

The same method closes the rest. Aliases live in hydro_plant_aliases.json; adding an entry and re-seeding pulls the plant in. Name matching alone cannot get there — it already took three passes to reach 90%.

How the numbers were derived

Fleet list
EPDK production licence export — 758 hydro plants after de-duplication, 34,437 MWm installed.
Classification
EPİAŞ realtime-generation splits output into river and dammedHydro per plant. Read directly, never inferred — a 50 MW size rule was tested and agreed with reality only 80% of the time.
Location
The licence file has no coordinates, only province and district. Plants sit at district centroids and are aggregated per district, so the map never implies precision it does not have.
Generation
24 Sep 2023 – 23 Sep 2026, one row per plant per day, from EPİAŞ realtime-generation — metered output, not the day-ahead schedule. Backfill in progress; the three-year history is what classified 45 plants a 30-day window could not.
Refresh
Scheduled daily at 06:00 for the previous day.
